# Goals Club Badge Design Reference

This document provides design specifications for all badges in The Goals Club. These badges are designed to be **collectable** like Japanese/Chinese collectables (Sonny Angel, Upsettduck, Funko Pop) featuring cute UK countryside animals in kawaii/chibi style.

---

## 🎨 Design Guidelines

### Overall Style
- **Kawaii/Chibi style**: Big heads, small bodies, adorable expressions
- **UK Countryside Animals**: Each category has a mascot animal
- **Collectable feel**: Users should want to "catch 'em all"
- **Accessories**: Animals wear/hold items related to the achievement
- **Consistent sizing**: 128x128px base, scalable SVG format
- **Circular badge frame** with coloured border matching category

### Brand Mascot: Gizmo the Pygmy Goat 🐐
**Gizmo** is the Goals Club ambassador - a cute pygmy goat with attitude (GOAT = Greatest Of All Time). Gizmo appears on special/prestigious badges and represents the brand.
- Small but mighty
- Cheeky expression with confidence
- Often wears a tiny crown or cape

## 🏷️ Ribbon Text Guidelines

Use short, punchy text on badge ribbons. Avoid words like "logs" or "logged" - use achievement-focused language instead:

| Category | Progression Pattern | Examples |
|----------|---------------------|----------|
| Goal Joining | "Xst/nd/rd GOAL" or "X GOALS" | "1st GOAL", "5 GOALS", "10 GOALS" |
| Activity Logging | "X ACTIVITY" or "X ACTIVITIES" | "1st ACTIVITY", "25 ACTIVITIES", "50 ACTIVITIES", "100 CLUB" |
| Goal Completion | "X GOAL COMPLETED" or "X GOALS COMPLETED" | "1 GOAL COMPLETED", "5 GOALS COMPLETED", "PERFECT 10" |
| Streaks | Time period | "7 DAYS", "4 WEEKS", "3 MONTHS" |
| Location | Milestone counts | "1 PEAK", "50 PEAKS", "214 MASTER" |

## 🐾 Animal Design Notes

### General Kawaii/Chibi Guidelines
- **Head-to-body ratio**: 1:1 or head slightly larger
- **Eyes**: Large, sparkly, expressive (anime-style)
- **Limbs**: Short, stubby, cute
- **Expressions**: Exaggerated emotions (joy, determination, pride)
- **Blush marks**: Optional pink cheeks for extra cuteness
- **Sparkles**: Add sparkle effects for achievements

### Gizmo the Pygmy Goat (Brand Mascot)
- Small, compact body with attitude
- Cheeky, confident expression
- Small horns, floppy ears
- Often has one eyebrow raised
- Signature colours: Gold and purple for special badges

### Progression Styling
Within each category, the same animal evolves:
1. **Tier 1**: Simple accessories, excited/new expression
2. **Tier 2**: More accessories, confident expression
3. **Tier 3+**: Premium accessories (crowns, capes), triumphant expression

## 📁 File Naming Convention

All badge files should be saved as:
```
/badges/{badge-slug}.svg
```

Examples:
- `/badges/founding-member.svg`
- `/badges/first-steps.svg`
- `/badges/wainwright-master.svg`
- `/badges/nt-completionist.svg`
